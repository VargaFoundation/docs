Use assistants from claude.ai, MCP clients and your own application

An assistant your organisation installed for you is not limited to the console. Two doors open the same conversations, in your personal space: the MCP door, for claude.ai, Claude Code and any other MCP client, and an embedded component, for a panel inside an internal web application. The assistant, its sources and its budget stay on the Choregos side. At acme, the billing assistant is reachable from claude.ai and from the finance team's intranet.

What you will be able to do: list and ask assistants from an MCP client, follow an answer that takes time, send a person to confirm a write, and frame an assistant in your application.

Before you start:

  • an account in the audience of at least one ready assistant;
  • for an MCP client: the connection of Connect your AI assistant (MCP). A token with mcp:read is enough to converse: a conversation reads, and its writes are proposals;
  • for the embed: an owner of the installation to list your application's origin.

From an MCP client​

The tools​

On https://choregos.example.com/mcp, three tools are announced only to people who are in the audience of at least one ready assistant:

ToolWhat it does
list_assistantsthe assistants you may talk to, as org:slug, with what each one does
ask_assistantsends a message to one assistant (assistant as org:slug, message, and optionally conversation_id to continue, and a context object)
get_conversationreads a conversation by its conversation_id

Each installation is also its own connector, with its own address:

https://choregos.example.com/mcp/assistants/acme:billing

There the tools are ask and get_conversation, and the assistant's own MCP prompts (a ready-made question with arguments) are served as MCP prompts. Outside the audience, that address answers 404. Choregos exposes its assistants only, never the tools of other servers.

A conversation opened here is one of your conversations in the console: you can continue it there.

Read an answer​

An answer carries conversation_id, status, text, citations, pending_actions and missing_accounts.

  • A turn that takes longer than 25 seconds answers status: working and goes on in the background. Call get_conversation with the conversation_id until the status is completed (or failed).
  • citations are the documents of the assistant's knowledge it used, with their title and version.
  • pending_actions are the writes it proposes and you have not decided. Each has a confirm_url.
  • missing_accounts are the accounts it needed and you have not connected, each with a connect_url.

Confirm a write​

The door never decides. When the assistant proposes a write, open its confirm_url: it leads to the console, where you sign in and press Confirm or Refuse. The write is made with your identity.

Connect it from claude.ai​

claude.ai calls from Anthropic's network and signs in with OAuth, so the conditions of claude.ai apply: a public HTTPS address and OAuth on the door, set by your operator. Then, in claude.ai under Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector, enter the assistant's address above and your administrator's client ID, and sign in with your usual identity provider.

Expect two models in cascade, the client's and the assistant's: more cost and latency than a skill kept in the client, in exchange for budget, audit, versions and evals. The assistant's MCP prompts save round trips.

What you should see: in claude.ai, the assistant answers with its citations; a write comes back as a link, never as an action done.

Embed an assistant in your application​

A host page shows the assistant in an iframe. The host holds no token, no model and no transcript: the person signs in to Choregos, and the conversation is theirs.

  1. Ask an owner of the installation to list your application's origin in the installation's config.embed_origins: origins only (https://intranet.acme.example), no path, no *, and http only for localhost.
  2. Put the frame in your page:
<iframe title="Billing assistant" width="480" height="520"
  src="https://choregos.example.com/embed/assistants/acme/billing?context=BASE64URL_JSON"></iframe>
  1. Optionally pass a context: a JSON object, base64url-encoded, that tells the assistant what the screen shows (for example the invoice open on it). Choregos checks it against the assistant's surfaces.embed.context_schema and gives it to the agent as data, never as an instruction. An assistant that declares no schema refuses a context.
  2. Listen to the frame's events (below).

Only the origins you listed may frame the page: the console answers with a frame-ancestors rule built from them, and every other console page refuses framing.

Sign-in​

If the frame has no session, it asks the identity provider once, silently. If the provider needs the person, the frame shows a Sign in button that opens the provider in a popup. The popup hands the frame a frame token that lasts 15 minutes, is bound to the person, their browser session and this installation, and opens only what the component calls. It stays in the frame and never reaches your page. So the host may be on another site than the console: no third-party cookie is needed. When the token expires, the button shows again.

Events​

The frame sends messages to your page with postMessage, only to the origin that framed it. Check event.origin (the console's) and event.data.source === "choregos.assistant". Each message is { source, type, org, installation, ...payload }:

typeWhenPayload
conversation.startedthe conversation is open, new or resumedconversation_id, resumed
action.confirmedthe person confirmed a writeconversation_id, action_id, operation, status
memory.updateda turn wrote or proposed in a memory space; add ?memory=space1,space2 to the frame to narrow itconversation_id, spaces
resizethe content's height changed: size your iframe to itheight, in pixels

The frame also shows the writes the assistant proposes, with what they would change, and Confirm and Refuse buttons.

What you should see: the assistant in your page, a conversation.started event in your listener, and the same conversation in the console under Assistants, My conversations.

What can go wrong​

SymptomLikely cause
the three tools are not announcedyou are in no audience, or no assistant is ready
404 on /mcp/assistants/…you are outside that assistant's audience, or the installation does not exist
the frame is blank, the console reports a framing refusalyour origin is not in config.embed_origins
the frame says the context is refused (422)it does not match context_schema, or the assistant declares none
the Sign in button shows againthe frame token expired, or your session ended
status: working never endscall get_conversation again; a failed status says the turn did not complete
